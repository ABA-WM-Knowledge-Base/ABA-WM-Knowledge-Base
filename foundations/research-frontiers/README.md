# World-Model Research Frontiers

## Retrieval metadata

**Relevant queries:** unresolved world-model questions, evidence gaps, scaling limits, causal abstraction, compositional generalization, uncertainty, long-horizon prediction, or evaluation validity.

**Knowledge provided:** cross-cutting unknowns whose resolution could change a modeling judgment, together with links to the canonical concepts and evidence needed to test them.

**Related pages:** all other [Foundation subparts](../README.md) own established concepts; [representative papers](../../papers/README.md) own work-specific evidence; [Cosmos3-Nano research registry](../../models/cosmos3-nano/research-queue.md) owns model-specific unknowns.

## Canonical boundary

This subpart owns unresolved, field-level questions and their decision consequences. It does not duplicate known mechanisms, turn hypotheses into facts, rank AIBuildAI work, or act as an execution queue.
